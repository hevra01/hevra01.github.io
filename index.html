About

I am a first-year PhD student in the Visual Intelligence & Perception Lab (VIP), supervised by Jan Eric Lenssen.

Before starting my PhD, I completed my Master's degree at Saarland University. During my Master's, I worked as a research assistant at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics and the Computer Vision and Machine Perception Lab. I completed my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at Middle East Technical University Northern Cyprus Campus.
